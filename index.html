Safety comes first.

KEVO should feel welcoming without asking anyone to give up their privacy.

KEVO safety principles
✓No personal direct messages inside KEVO.
✓No real-person profile photos; use expressive illustrated avatars instead.
✓No follower counts, popularity rankings or pressure to collect likes.
✓Never share your address, school, phone number, passwords or live location with strangers.
✓People should be able to leave a conversation and seek help from a trusted adult if something feels uncomfortable.
✓A real public community needs working reporting, blocking, moderation and privacy controls before launch.
Important: This is a front-end prototype, not a live social network. It has no accounts, server, public posting, messaging or moderation system. We must build and test those safety systems before allowing strangers to interact.
